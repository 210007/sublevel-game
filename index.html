SUBLEVEL

A corporate-dystopia dungeon crawler with a gameshow twist: descend floor by floor, loot and level up, and fight your way through whatever the broadcast throws at you next. Procedurally generated floors, no two runs alike.

  • Procedural floors, seeded & replayable
  • Loot tiers from common to legendary
  • Boss fights with multiple AI archetypes
  • Ranged and exploder enemy variants
  • Environmental hazards across the floors
  • Sprint/stamina, earnable support drone, skills & stats
Download for Windows Windows desktop build
Early build — think of it as a vertical slice, not a finished game. No install needed, just run the .exe. Nothing is collected; no account required.
